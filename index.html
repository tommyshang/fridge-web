Privacy Policy — Fridge

Last updated: 2026-09-03

Fridge ("the app") is a fridge/pantry inventory tracker. This policy explains what data the app touches and how it is used.

Data the app collects

The app does not require an account, does not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not transmit your inventory data to any server.

What we do not do

  • No user accounts, sign-in, or personal identifiers collected
  • No advertising or ad tracking
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No sale of data to third parties
  • No data leaves your device except the anonymous barcode lookup described above

Permissions used

  • Camera — to scan barcodes and photograph items/expiration dates
  • Photo library — to attach an existing photo to an inventory item
  • Notifications — to remind you before food expires

Data retention & deletion

All inventory data lives in a local database on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes all locally stored data. There is no server-side account to delete.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised.
